# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-06)

First release.

- Review Anki cards in WoW Forever: new, learning and review cards from chosen decks, Anki's interval labels.
- Reviews are applied in Anki with Anki's scheduler (FSRS or SM-2) and the time you answered in-game.
- Japanese text (bundled Noto Sans JP), furigana display options, images (TGA) and audio (mp3/ogg).
- Undo, suspend, bury, flags, mark, add notes in-game.
- Auto-open on flight paths / death / inns, hide in combat, keyboard shortcuts and key bindings, minimap button.
- AnkiCraft Bridge (Anki add-on): auto-sync while Anki is open, one undo step per sync, conflict detection
  when a card was reviewed elsewhere, Windows file-lock handling.
